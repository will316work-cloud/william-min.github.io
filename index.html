About Me

[ Profile
Picture ]

As a technical game designer, I design and build games that are easy to pick up but difficult to put down. I specialize in turning simple mechanics into deep, emergent player experiences. Whether playing, watching, or designing, I analyze how individual systems combine to create something greater than the sum of their parts.

Mechanically minded and technically grounded, I use Unity, C#, and generative AI to rapidly prototype and build scalable, flexible architecture that empowers smooth content production.

When I don't dissect and build game loops, I draw, cook, or sometimes go skiing or golding with friends.

Hobbies & Interests 📌

    Drawing & Sketching

    • It allows me to visually communicate ideas, level design, and technical architecture.
    • I train my sense of color theory and shape language to naturally guide viewer eyes.

    Cooking

    • I experiment with extra and alternative condiments, toppings, and oils out of necessity or curiosity to test if the changes enhance the taste or replicate it.
    • I practice preparation, routine, and growing efficiency in my process.
    • I enjoy completing something to consume within shorter and shorter times.

    Skiing

    • I understand the joy of gaining a sense of momentum and flow with solid execution, much like achieving game feel after learning the fundamentals and essential techniques.
    • I love making real-time, micro-decisions down the hill where routing, speed, and environmental adaptation dictate the success of the run.

Golfing

  • I have seen the amount of technique and strategy that is put behind every swing from the driver to the putter, which encapsulates the depth possible from a simple action.
  • It allows me to thrive in a disciplined environment of constant minor adjustments, accommodation for environmental factors, and parsing feedback loops to perfect execution.
